10. Laboratory Department Management
Each department should have its own dashboard and workflow.
Department	Main functionality
Haematology	CBC, ESR, blood cell analysis
Biochemistry	LFT, KFT, glucose, lipid profile
Microbiology	Culture, sensitivity, organism identification
Serology	Antigen and antibody testing
Immunology	Hormone and immunological tests
Clinical Pathology	Urine, stool and body fluid analysis
Histopathology	Tissue processing and examination
Cytology	Cellular examination
Molecular Diagnostics	PCR and molecular tests
Radiology	X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound
Cardiology	ECG, Echo and related investigations

Department-specific work queues, test assignment, result entry, quality checks and reporting should be supported.
